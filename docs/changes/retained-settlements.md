# Retained latest settlement outcomes

- Status: proposed; Q1-Q2 below need user decisions before implementation.
- Scope: next [C3 conversion](core-v4-review.md#user-decisions-2026-11-12), core/protocol/broker/KDE together. Preserve retained focus and intent, terminal predicates, FIFO commands and raw evidence. No acknowledged-events lane, new delivery sequence, retry policy or C4 attempt unification.
- Model: [retained intent](retained-intent-desire.md), with outcomes rather than storage targets. Settlement rows describe completed core accounting; they never authorize native writes or prove more native completion than their existing payload.

## Proposed retained replacement

- Keep the required top-level names `gestureSettled`, `operationSettled`, `switchSettled`; change their meaning to complete current outcome projections, never take-once queues. Bootstrap is null/empty. Core retains typed values; wire encoding stays in protocol.
- `gestureSettled` remains one global nullable `{id,committed}` slot per `(adapter,gen)`. Each newly incorporated terminal replaces it; consumed/stale replay cannot regress its identity or replace its original `committed` value. This needs explicit producer guards: today's unconditional recorder and stale Esc/unmatched-terminal paths can overwrite the slot (`C/world_engine.rs:2289-2297`, `C/world_gesture.rs:116-128,237-253,285-299`). Preserve consumption and active-gesture cancellation semantics; do not suppress a genuine first terminal merely because no gesture bound. Q1 decides start-time clearing. Neither an unchanged snapshot nor setter acceptance fabricates completion.
- `operationSettled` becomes rows `{win,kind,op,outcome,cause}`, unique by `(win,kind)`, with closed `kind:"clear"|"float"`. The latest terminal for each phase replaces only that slot. Preserve the existing outcome/cause vocabulary and validate kind/outcome agreement. Keep both phases: a clear can resume a float under the **same** operation id (`C/world_clear.rs:74-80`), so uniqueness by `op` or `win` alone loses meaning. No operation-id parsing.
- `switchSettled` becomes rows `{out,op,outcome,failed,focus,fallback}`, unique by owner `out`. This is the output key passed to the existing terminal stager (`C/world_selection.rs:1691-1699`), not a claim that the switch affects only one output. Preserve parent correlation, frozen failed members, focusless completion and refusal semantics; no new switch id or synthetic superseded-success outcome. Q1 also decides whether admitting newer same-key work clears an older terminal.
- Retain operation outcomes through held/minimized/incomplete omissions and switch outcomes through held outputs. Q2 decides what remains after proven owner departure/close; do not silently erase a close terminal or publish it just once before pruning. A removed owner's outcome is not authority over a replacement lifetime; absence proves no particular verdict. Gesture outcome is generation-scoped, including after its subject closes. Fresh handshake/new generation clears all owners; no restart persistence or replay into replacement native lifetimes.
- Project in stable key order without mutation. Repeated/consumed commands and commandless publications retain outcomes. Different keys cannot erase one another; newer terminals on the same key win even when their predecessors were never delivered. No guarantee of every historical terminal: current outcome state, not an event log. Q1 specifies the additional start-time supersession boundary.
- Preserve the existing **1,024 operation rows, 1,024 switch rows** (`C/world_report.rs:2560-2562`) and 4 MiB reply bounds. The live switch map naturally has at most one slot per output (16); that is not the wire section cap. Budget the entire retained projection, including failed lists, any Q2 departed summaries and reservations for admitted pending work. Two phase slots share the operation cap, not 10,000 window slots: retaining outcomes can therefore refuse new keys at 1,024 occupied/reserved slots even across many reports. Reserve before admission so later settlement cannot overflow an accepted obligation; reject overbound candidates transactionally, never truncate or silently evict unrelated outcomes. Pending reservations are sizing, not another delivery owner.

## Adapter completion and broker association

- A gesture completes adapter-local waiting only when the latest retained terminal matches its exact pending gesture identity and current binding. Re-emission is inert after local retirement. A later gesture's terminal must not finish an older or newer unrelated flight. Retain existing core gesture consumption and native FIFO acknowledgement; neither is a new publication-acknowledgement lane.
- KDE cannot simply keep the value and reuse the issuing flight: current discharge requires `flight.gesture` and the current capture's `gestureAssoc` (`K/world-entry.ts:4466-4489`); the broker injects association from **this capture's** normalized ingress (`B/world_broker.rs:884-891`). Idle/unavailable ingress has no association (`B/kde_gesture.rs:80-90,246-252`).
- Add required nullable broker-envelope `gestureSettledAssoc:{instance,serial,coreId}`, distinct from current-ingress `gestureAssoc`. Accepted replies carry the retained terminal's association; broker rejection/connection-error envelopes require it null, changing both exact-key readers (`K/world-report.ts:1103-1118`, `K/world-connection.ts:194-239`). No native identity enters core's payload.
- Pin **both** the currently retained core gesture's originating association and the latest terminal association (at most two tuples), independently of the evictable normalization map. A close with omitted ingress can settle an older active gesture (`C/world_gesture.rs:269-284`); 256 unbindable newcomers can evict its mapping without replacing core's gesture (`B/kde_gesture.rs:444-448`, `C/world_gesture.rs:145-188`). Use a typed read-only core active-gesture-id accessor, snapshot its verified mapping before normalization can evict it, and reconcile pins only after accepted transitions. Normalized ingress alone is not proof that core bound the newcomer. Resolve the terminal from the matching active pin or its verified newly incorporated ingress; retain that terminal pin until replacement/reset. Never recover identity from script ids, prefixes or a later unrelated ingress.
- Discharge compares the retained association/core terminal with the **currently pending native FIFO head**, not `flight.gesture`. Keep exact current-generation/adapter and response request/source/nativeInvalid binding checks. The existing acknowledgement-only path may discharge a causally bound terminal even when its accompanying snapshot is stale; it still applies no desires or paint. Duplicate delivery and late ack callbacks remain exact-identity/idempotent.
- Route every settlement consumer through that same check, including `ackGestureTerminal`, the in-apply path and drop diagnostics (`K/world-entry.ts:4372-4400,4730-4744,4982-5000`). An idle capture must not clear an older unresolved terminal-capture retry merely because its own `flight.gesture` is null (`:4417-4438,6044-6069`); resolve only the matching pending obligation. Preserve existing retry policy and exact-envelope recovery, not its obsolete issuing-only assumptions.
- Same-generation transport reconnect preserves the terminal and its pinned association; a fresh handshake clears them together and follows existing native FIFO recovery, never relabels old ids. Apply-only-latest journeys must include commandless/idle ingress, not warm the adapter with the issuing reply. If an originating native association is unavailable, publish no fabricated association and withhold native retirement truthfully.
- Operation/switch completion is already core-derived from raw evidence. Retained outcomes do not remove raw evidence queues, unspend writes, release arrival barriers or create new native cleanup consumers. The next full desired state remains authority even if every intermediate terminal was skipped.

## Replace, do not layer; amendment boundary

- Replace `gesture_terminal.take()`, operation/switch drain vectors and comments promising issuing-only output with the three retained owners; terminal producers update those owners directly. Replace event-only sizing reservations with whole-map/pending-slot budgeting and stale save-once expectations with retention assertions. Keep core consumption, retained pending operations/selections, first-evidence latches, command dedup, raw attempt receipt handling and native ack retry/freshness fences.
- KDE replaces issuing-flight-only gesture settlement matching; add strict accepted/rejected envelope validation and bounded active/terminal association handling in the production broker and genuine test broker. Delete obsolete "core never re-emits" assumptions, not native FIFO ownership or its exact-identity ack. Operation/switch readers change keys; they gain no actuation authority.
- In-place pre-release v4 amendment: core/protocol/broker/KDE change together, including required keys, duplicate-key validation, exact/upper-bound sizing and bootstrap replies. No alias, dual shape or version bump. Update the WorldReply, raw-outcome and recovery sections in the contract and relevant decisions only after approval.
- Refresh active wire witnesses through genuine codecs and behavioral fixtures through genuine engine/entry recorders, including `world-wire/v4-active`, `stage5-v4-active/{full,s2}` and `kde-v4-active/{bytes,migration}`. Preserve frozen `world-wire/v4`, `stage5`, `stage5-v4` and historical `kde-v4` byte-for-byte; keep both stage5 proofs enabled. Never hand-patch producer replies.
- macOS must adopt the typed retained shape, consume matching gesture completion from later accepted replies without requiring the issuing ticket, and fence supersession/restart/late callbacks; `M/world_dispatch.rs:592-594` currently forwards the applied reply's gesture outcome. No operation/switch actuator is added. Windows adopts with v4: current legacy `W/tiling_sys/world_tick.rs:114-134` clears a stashed override only on matching terminal id, while operation outcomes are diagnostic. Legacy `WorldReply` is not evidence of v4 adoption. Neither port gains native acceptance from KDE offline proofs.
